# Hearth 1.0.4

- Added **Settings → Startup & closing** with two optional preferences, both off by default.
- **Close to system tray** keeps Hearth running when you close the window. Double-click the tray icon to reopen it, or use **Open Hearth** and **Quit Hearth** from its menu.
- **Start Hearth with Windows** launches Hearth when your Windows account signs in. With close to tray also enabled, it starts quietly in the tray.
- The independent hosting service continues running when you close or quit the launcher. Launcher startup preferences do not change hosting.
- Updates preserve your profile and startup preference. Uninstall removes automatic startup for the normal launcher profile.

Update through Settings → Updates → Check for updates, then Download update and Restart to update.
